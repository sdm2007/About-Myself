## 🔨 Things I've Built

### 💧 Shiyank: IoT Water Quality Tester

A team prototype of a water-testing device. Water runs through a pipe past four probes (pH, TDS, turbidity and temperature), and an **ESP32** reads them and shows the results on an OLED display. The firmware runs a three-state sample cycle (no sample, settling, measuring) and the whole circuit runs in the Wokwi simulator. My portfolio also has an interactive 3D model of it.

It is an educational screening prototype, not a certified drinking-water safety device.

**Team:** Disha Saini, Manshi Dwivedi, Purnima Rajwade, Shivani Dhama, Jyoti Mishra  
**Technologies:** C++ (Arduino) • ESP32 • DS18B20 • SSD1306 OLED • Wokwi  
🔗 [Wokwi simulation](https://wokwi.com/projects/479492563395301377)

### 🛒 jamGhat: Hyperlocal Marketplace

A web app for finding local shops, checking stock and prices, and getting the shortest route, with no account needed for shoppers. Shop owners register, verify their email and manage their own listing.

- 🔍 Search across shops, categories and items
- 📍 Live GPS, nearby-shop map and shortest route (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap)
- 💰 Market price range comparing the same item across shops
- 📦 Stock labels worked out from quantity
- 🛠️ Owner dashboard, email verification and admin approval of new shops
- 🌐 English and Hindi interface

I wrote all the code. **Jyoti Mishra** drafted the terms and conditions and privacy policy and handled marketing.  
**Technologies:** React • Vite • Tailwind CSS • Node.js • Express • Supabase (PostgreSQL) • Render  
🔗 [Live app](https://jamghat.thesrock.in) • [Source](https://github.com/sdm2007/jamghat)
